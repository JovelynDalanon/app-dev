# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## 📌 Overview
**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi mystery series that follows a group of kids in the 1980s as they uncover strange experiments and a dark parallel world called the *Upside Down*.

---

## ⭐ Main Characters
- **Eleven** – A girl with psychokinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler** – Leader of the friend group  
- **Will Byers** – The boy who gets trapped in the Upside Down  
- **Dustin Henderson** – Funny, smart, and loyal  
- **Lucas Sinclair** – Brave and rational  
- **Jim Hopper** – The town’s police chief  

---

## 🔥 Why I Like It
> *It mixes mystery, nostalgia, horror, comedy, and friendship in one amazing story.*

- Great soundtrack  
- Interesting plot twists  
- Strong character development
